STEP 2
Personal consultation

We know that every mental heath issue is different. That’s why we provide a personal consultation with our rehab experts to build a treatment plan that is tailored to your specific circumstances and personal requirements.

Learn More
Video therapy sessions

You can schedule one-on-one video sessions with a licensed therapist from the comfort of your home. Whether you prefer talking through your feelings or need guidance on specific challenges, these sessions are designed to give you the support you need, just like in-person therapy.

Anytime messaging support

Whenever you need to talk but can’t wait for your next session, you can message a therapist or even connect with peer support groups. You don’t have to be in crisis to reach out—this is just a way to stay connected and get ongoing support.

Self-guided therapy modules

We’ve designed a set of interactive modules that you can go through at your own pace. These modules cover everything from managing anxiety to improving your sleep or developing coping strategies.

Crisis support and emergency help

If you’re ever in crisis, you can reach out for immediate support. Whether it’s a live chat or video call with a trained crisis counselor, we’re here for you when things feel overwhelming.

Mindfulness and meditation practices

Whenever you need a moment to decompress, you can access a variety of guided meditation and mindfulness practices. These are perfect for reducing stress, calming anxiety, and improving your overall well-being.
